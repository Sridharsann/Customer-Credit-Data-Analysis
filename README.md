Credit Card & Customer Analytics

Interactive Excel Dashboard Project;

An end-to-end Excel analytics project focused on analysing credit card transactions, revenue performance, credit risk, customer demographics and financial behaviour.

The project uses data preparation, analytical calculations and interactive dashboards to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

Tools & Technologies;

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Power Pivot
- PivotTables & PivotCharts
- DAX
- Slicers and KPI Cards

Project Dashboards;

1. Credit Card Transaction Analysis

Analyses transaction trends, payment methods, interest earned and credit card limits.

2. Credit Card Revenue & Risk Analysis

Evaluates interest revenue, delinquency rates, credit utilisation and revolving balances.

3. Customer Segmentation & Financial Profiling

Explores customer demographics, income patterns, occupation and customer satisfaction.

4. Customer Value, Behaviour & Risk Intelligence

Examines customer acquisition cost, activation rates, spending patterns and ownership characteristics.

Workbook Structure;

The Excel workbook contains:

- 4 interactive dashboards
- 5 calculation and supporting analysis sheets

Project Files;

- Excel workbook – Complete dashboard and calculation sheets.
- Dashboard previews – Visual representation of all four dashboards.
